# German A1–B1 route / Немецкий маршрут

42 practical topics for life and work in Germany and Austria: 14 at each level. Start with setup; the tutor adapts the order to observed skills and priorities. Topic numbers are identifiers, not required lesson numbers. A topic can take several lessons, and a diagnostic can reveal already usable skills.

42 практические темы для жизни и работы в Германии и Австрии. Номер темы не равен номеру занятия. Карту не нужно проходить как список грамматических правил: задача — научиться действовать в конкретной ситуации.

## How to use this map

Follow each card’s prerequisites as skills to check, not an inflexible schedule. If the learner lacks a prerequisite, add a short bridge or choose another ready topic. Mixed review runs alongside new learning. All current learner states and thematic chat titles belong in `courses/current/TOPICS.md`; the index below defines content and default titles only.

Grammar supports communication. A polite fixed expression may appear before its full grammar explanation. Teach standard German, useful pronunciation and repair phrases; do not expect dialect comprehension. Useful regional recognition examples: `Jänner` (Austria) / `Januar`, `Sackerl` / `Tüte`, `Semmel` / `Brötchen`, and `Grüß Gott` as a greeting in Austria and parts of southern Germany. Present these briefly in relevant situations, not as universal regional rules. Standard forms remain valid learning targets.

Health, housing and administration cards are fictional language practice, not medical or legal advice. Verify current local procedures from appropriate official sources if a learner asks a real-world factual question. Work examples can use software/KI, but B1 goals remain simple familiar explanations, not specialist debate or technical fluency.
