# DE-A1-07 — Shopping and prices

- **Can-do:** Ask for an item, its size or amount, and its price.
- **Situation:** A supermarket counter or clothing shop.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Was kostet das?; Haben Sie das in Größe …?; Ich nehme …; Das ist zu teuer.; Kann ich mit Karte zahlen?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Accusative articles in frequent chunks; plural/quantities; demonstrative das; simple adjectives.
- **Listening goal:** A clear short exchange: identify quantity and total price, including a simple correction.
- **Speaking goal:** Ask two product questions, choose an item and confirm payment.
- **Practical success:** Buy a suitable item and correctly confirm quantity/price; identify the corrected total from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-02, DE-A1-06.
- **Return to review:** Quantity/price misunderstandings recur or the learner cannot check a corrected total. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
