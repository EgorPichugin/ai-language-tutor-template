# DE-A1-03 — Family and people

- **Can-do:** Describe close people and understand a simple description.
- **Situation:** Show a fictional family photo to a classmate.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Das ist meine Schwester.; Er ist … Jahre alt.; Wir wohnen zusammen.; Hast du Geschwister?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** mein/dein; er/sie/wir; haben; basic plural nouns and predicate adjectives.
- **Listening goal:** A slow 20–30-second description: identify the relationship and age.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe two people in four short sentences and ask one family question; allow fictional people.
- **Practical success:** Give relationship plus two facts and respond to a family question; identify 2 audio details.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-01, DE-A1-02.
- **Return to review:** Possessives or pronouns repeatedly obscure who is being described. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
