# DE-A1-12 — First days at work

- **Can-do:** Say what you do and ask for a simple work task.
- **Situation:** A new colleague explains a basic daily duty.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich arbeite als …; Ich bin neu hier.; Was soll ich machen?; Ich bin fertig.; Können Sie das zeigen?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Present verbs; als + occupation; sollen/können; task verb chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A slow colleague instruction: identify task and start time.
- **Speaking goal:** Introduce a role, clarify one instruction and report completion.
- **Practical success:** Exchange role/task/time information and signal completion; retrieve task/time from audio. Write a two-line completion message separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-05, DE-A1-11.
- **Return to review:** A key task is misunderstood or the learner cannot ask what to do next without a model. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A1-12 — First days at work`
