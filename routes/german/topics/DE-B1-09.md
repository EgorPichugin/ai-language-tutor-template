# DE-B1-09 — A basic job interview

- **Can-do:** Answer familiar interview questions and ask about a role.
- **Situation:** An entry-level interview in a known work area.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich interessiere mich für diese Stelle, weil …; Ein Beispiel dafür ist …; Ich möchte mich in … weiterentwickeln.; Wie sieht … aus?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Question formation; reasons; hypothetical würde; review experience narration.
- **Listening goal:** Clearly delivered interview questions and role description: identify duties, schedule and next step.
- **Speaking goal:** Introduce experience, give a skill example, answer a follow-up and ask two relevant questions.
- **Practical success:** Complete a six- to eight-turn basic interview using real or explicitly fictional experience; identify gist and 3 job facts from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-B1-08, DE-B1-02, DE-B1-05.
- **Return to review:** A simple unprepared follow-up causes a breakdown or the learner cannot relate a claimed skill to an example. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-B1-09 — A basic job interview`
