# DE-B1-14 — An independent everyday and work task

- **Can-do:** Combine a connected account, a practical decision and cooperative planning.
- **Situation:** Explain a familiar project delay, compare two next steps and agree a plan.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Bisher haben wir …; Es gibt zwei Möglichkeiten.; Ich würde …, weil …; Wir haben vereinbart, dass …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Recycle narrative, reasons, comparisons, conditions and summary forms; no new grammar.
- **Listening goal:** Two clear standard messages: identify main problem, options, decision and responsibility.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a short explanation, discuss two familiar options and answer an unprepared follow-up; confirm a plan in writing.
- **Practical success:** Complete linked interaction, listening, reading, writing and brief mediation samples with separate evidence; retrieve gist plus 3 details from audio. Completion is internal route evidence only.
