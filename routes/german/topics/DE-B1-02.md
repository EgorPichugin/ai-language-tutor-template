# DE-B1-02 — Opinions with reasons

- **Can-do:** Express an opinion on a familiar issue and respond to another view.
- **Situation:** Discuss remote work, commuting or learning habits with a friend.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Meiner Meinung nach …; Ein Grund dafür ist …; Ich verstehe deinen Punkt, aber …; Zum Beispiel …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Weil/denn/deshalb; concessive obwohl in simple clauses; opinion chunks; clause linking.
- **Listening goal:** A clear 60–90-second opinion: identify position, two reasons and one example.
- **Speaking goal:** State a view, give two short reasons and respond to one different view without a prepared speech.
- **Practical success:** Sustain a short familiar discussion with reasons/example; identify gist and 3 supporting details. No extended abstract debate required.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-05, DE-A2-12.
- **Return to review:** Reasons are repeated as slogans without explanation or a different view causes a return to scripted answers. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
