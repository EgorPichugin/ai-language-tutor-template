# DE-B1-11 — Explaining a KI project

- **Can-do:** Describe what a familiar AI tool does and one practical limitation.
- **Situation:** Explain a fictional text-sorting assistant to a colleague.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Das System erkennt …; Es kann Fehler machen.; Deshalb prüfen wir …; Die Daten werden … gespeichert.; Es soll … unterstützen.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Simple passive for recognition/practice in a familiar chunk; modal verbs; cause/effect; plain paraphrase.
- **Listening goal:** A clear project explanation: identify input, output and the stated need for human checking.
- **Speaking goal:** Explain input/output/use and one limitation in everyday terms; respond to a practical question.
- **Practical success:** Explain a familiar use case and a concrete check without abstract technical debate; identify gist and 3 audio facts. Use fictional data, never secrets.
- **Dependencies:** DE-B1-10, DE-B1-02.
- **Return to review:** The learner cannot communicate the scenario's stated need to check outputs or explain its stated limitation in simple language. Supply these facts as scenario context; assess language, not technical AI knowledge. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
