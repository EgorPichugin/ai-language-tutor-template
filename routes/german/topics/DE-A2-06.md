# DE-A2-06 — Phone calls and appointments

- **Can-do:** Handle a predictable call and leave a short message.
- **Situation:** Call a workplace reception to change a meeting.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Guten Tag, hier spricht …; Könnte ich bitte … sprechen?; Es geht um …; Können Sie bitte ausrichten, dass …?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Polite could as a chunk; dass clause in a short message; phone spelling; formal register.
- **Listening goal:** Audio-only clear slow call: identify caller, purpose and callback time without transcript.
- **Speaking goal:** Open a call, explain purpose, ask for repetition and leave a three-part message.
- **Practical success:** Relay caller/purpose/time accurately and close the call; recover those 3 details from audio. Text-only practice is not a listening pass.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-02, DE-A1-11.
- **Return to review:** Names or times repeatedly disappear when relaying the message, or the learner cannot request clarification. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
