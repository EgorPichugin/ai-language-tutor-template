# DE-A2-11 — Learning and work skills

- **Can-do:** Explain how you learned a practical skill.
- **Situation:** A course adviser or colleague asks about training.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich habe gelernt, … zu …; Zuerst musste ich …; Jetzt kann ich … besser.; Ich möchte noch … lernen.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Zu-infinitive patterns; common past modal musste/konnte; comparison of ability.
- **Listening goal:** A short clear training account: identify skill, learning method and remaining difficulty.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe one skill, how it was learned and one next goal.
- **Practical success:** Give a five- to six-sentence learning account and answer a follow-up; identify 3 audio facts.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-04, DE-A2-05.
- **Return to review:** The learner cannot connect the skill to a concrete example or repeatedly confuses past/current ability. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
