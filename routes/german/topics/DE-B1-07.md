# DE-B1-07 — Tasks and deadlines

- **Can-do:** Report task progress and negotiate a realistic deadline.
- **Situation:** A short team update on a familiar piece of work.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich bin mit … fertig.; Mir fehlt noch …; Bis Freitag schaffe ich …; Wenn …, dann …; Ich brauche Unterstützung bei …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Bis/seit; condition wenn; present/Perfekt; subordinate-clause order; time planning chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A clear team update: identify completed work, blocker and proposed deadline.
- **Speaking goal:** Give done/next/blocker information and negotiate one changed deadline.
- **Practical success:** Agree an achievable next step and owner/date in a short exchange; recover gist and 3 audio details.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-04, DE-B1-04, DE-B1-06.
- **Return to review:** Done vs planned work is confused again or a changed deadline cannot be explained. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
