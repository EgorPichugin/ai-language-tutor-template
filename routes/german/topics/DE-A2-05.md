# DE-A2-05 — Reasons and preferences

- **Can-do:** Explain a familiar choice with a simple reason.
- **Situation:** Choose a class, shift or weekend activity.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich möchte …, weil …; Deshalb …; Ich finde … gut.; Ich bin anderer Meinung, denn …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** weil + final verb; deshalb + verb-second; denn; review comparatives.
- **Listening goal:** A short slow conversation: identify each person’s choice and reason.
- **Speaking goal:** State a choice, give a reason and ask about the partner’s preference.
- **Practical success:** Explain two choices understandably and respond to one different preference; recover choice/reason from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-02, DE-A2-01.
- **Return to review:** Word order or reason connectors obscure cause and effect in two sessions. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
