# DE-A2-08 — Returns and service problems

- **Can-do:** Explain a simple purchase problem and ask for a remedy.
- **Situation:** Return a faulty headset or an incorrect order.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich habe das gestern gekauft.; Leider funktioniert es nicht.; Ich möchte es umtauschen.; Ist eine Rückgabe möglich?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Perfekt; object pronouns; möchten; reason clauses; service register.
- **Listening goal:** A short clear service reply: identify offered option and one condition.
- **Speaking goal:** Explain purchase/problem and ask for replacement or refund; respond to an offered alternative.
- **Practical success:** Reach a clear next step through four to six turns; identify remedy/condition from audio. No claims about legal entitlement.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-03, DE-A2-05, DE-A1-07.
- **Return to review:** Problem and remedy are confused or the same object-reference error prevents resolution again. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A2-08 — Returns and service problems`
