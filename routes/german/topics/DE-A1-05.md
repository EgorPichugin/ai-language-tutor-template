# DE-A1-05 — Daily routine

- **Can-do:** Describe an ordinary day and ask about another person’s routine.
- **Situation:** Small talk before a class or shift.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich stehe um … auf.; Dann fahre ich zur Arbeit.; Ich arbeite von … bis …; Abends koche ich.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Present tense; common separable verbs; verb-second order with time first; von/bis.
- **Listening goal:** A slow 30-second routine: identify two activities and their order.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe four steps in a day and ask one routine question; practise separable verb rhythm.
- **Practical success:** Give a comprehensible four-step sequence with time markers; order two heard actions correctly.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-04.
- **Return to review:** Separable verbs or verb placement repeatedly prevent a listener following the day. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
