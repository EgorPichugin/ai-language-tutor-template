# DE-B1-01 — Connected personal stories

- **Can-do:** Tell a connected familiar story and explain why it mattered.
- **Situation:** Describe a move, a trip or a first working day to a colleague.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Als ich …, …; Zuerst …, später …; Das war wichtig, weil …; Seitdem …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Als vs wenn in frequent contexts; Perfekt and common Präteritum; connectors and pronoun reference.
- **Listening goal:** A clear standard 60–90-second story: identify main event, sequence and reason.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a connected one- to two-minute account with an ending; answer a spontaneous follow-up.
- **Practical success:** Tell an understandable event sequence with one reason/reflection and a follow-up answer; identify gist plus 3 details from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-03, DE-A2-05, DE-A2-12.
- **Return to review:** The story becomes an unordered list again or a new event cannot be narrated without a model. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
