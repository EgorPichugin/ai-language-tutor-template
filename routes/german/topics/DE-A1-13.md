# DE-A1-13 — Free time and invitations

- **Can-do:** Invite someone to a simple activity and accept or decline.
- **Situation:** A colleague suggests a walk or coffee after work.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Hast du am Samstag Zeit?; Wollen wir …?; Ich komme gern.; Leider kann ich nicht.; Vielleicht am Sonntag?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** gern/lieber as chunks; wollen/können; time expressions; du/Sie appropriate to relationship.
- **Listening goal:** A slow invitation: identify activity, day and acceptance/refusal.
- **Speaking goal:** Invite, respond and offer an alternative time in a short exchange.
- **Practical success:** Agree an activity/time or politely decline; identify activity and decision from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-04, DE-A1-06.
- **Return to review:** Acceptance and refusal are confused or offering an alternative needs substantial help. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
