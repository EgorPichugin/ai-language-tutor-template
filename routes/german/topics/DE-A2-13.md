# DE-A2-13 — Messages and everyday administration

- **Can-do:** Understand a short appointment message and ask what is needed.
- **Situation:** A fictional appointment at a course office or local service desk.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Welche Unterlagen brauche ich?; Ich möchte den Termin bestätigen.; Im Brief steht, dass …; Können Sie mir das erklären?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Dass; question formation; müssen/sollen; frequent formal email opening/closing chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A short clear explanation: identify appointment purpose, time and required item.
- **Speaking goal:** Ask for clarification and relay the three essentials to another person in simple language.
- **Practical success:** Extract three facts from an original short notice, confirm a meeting and ask one question; separately identify 3 audio facts. No legal/immigration eligibility advice.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-06, DE-A1-02, DE-A2-05.
- **Return to review:** A required action is repeatedly missed or the learner cannot distinguish information from a request. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
