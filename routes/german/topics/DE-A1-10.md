# DE-A1-10 — Health and simple needs

- **Can-do:** State a simple symptom and request an appointment.
- **Situation:** A fictional call to a medical reception desk; language practice only.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich habe Kopfschmerzen.; Mir geht es nicht gut.; Ich brauche einen Termin.; Seit gestern.; Können Sie langsamer sprechen?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** haben + symptom; brauchen; seit gestern as a chunk; body words in phrases.
- **Listening goal:** A clear slow reception reply: identify offered day/time and where to wait.
- **Speaking goal:** State a fictional symptom, ask for an appointment and confirm arrangements.
- **Practical success:** Explain one symptom and book/confirm a time; identify 2 relevant audio facts. Do not diagnose or recommend treatment.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-04, DE-A1-01.
- **Return to review:** Symptom/time statements become unclear or the appointment can no longer be confirmed independently. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A1-10 — Health and simple needs`
