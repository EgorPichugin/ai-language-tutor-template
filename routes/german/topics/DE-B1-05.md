# DE-B1-05 — Phone calls and clarification

- **Can-do:** Manage a familiar phone call with one unexpected complication.
- **Situation:** A meeting is moved and the intended contact is unavailable.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Habe ich richtig verstanden, dass …?; Könnten Sie mir kurz erklären, …?; Ich fasse kurz zusammen.; Ich rufe später noch einmal an.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Indirect questions with ob/W-word; dass; polite requests; summary connectors.
- **Listening goal:** A clear standard audio-only call: identify changed plan, reason and action needed.
- **Speaking goal:** Clarify an ambiguous detail, negotiate a callback and summarize the agreement.
- **Practical success:** Resolve one misunderstanding and confirm the action/time; retrieve gist and 3 details without reading a transcript.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-06, DE-B1-04.
- **Return to review:** The learner accepts an unclear arrangement without checking or cannot recover from a changed detail. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
