# DE-B1-03 — Comparing options

- **Can-do:** Compare two familiar options and recommend one for a specific need.
- **Situation:** Choose a course, transport option or simple work tool.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Einerseits …, andererseits …; Im Vergleich zu …; Für mich ist … wichtiger.; Ich würde … wählen.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Comparatives/superlatives; würde + infinitive; connectors for simple contrast.
- **Listening goal:** A clear comparison: identify two options and the practical advantage of each.
- **Speaking goal:** Compare cost/time/usability with two criteria and make a recommendation with a reason.
- **Practical success:** Make a choice, link it to the stated needs and explain two comparisons; identify gist and 3 audio details.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-01, DE-B1-02.
- **Return to review:** The learner lists features but cannot connect them to a choice in a changed situation. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-B1-03 — Comparing options`
