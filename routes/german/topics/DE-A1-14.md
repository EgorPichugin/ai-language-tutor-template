# DE-A1-14 — An everyday day out

- **Can-do:** Combine simple travel, shopping and meeting arrangements.
- **Situation:** Meet a friend near a station and buy a snack.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Zuerst …, dann …; Wir treffen uns um …; Ich brauche noch …; Entschuldigung, wo ist …?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Recycle present, modal verbs, basic questions and time phrases; introduce no new grammar.
- **Listening goal:** Two short slow messages: identify meeting place/time and a changed detail.
- **Speaking goal:** Manage three short linked exchanges and ask for clarification once if needed.
- **Practical success:** Reach the meeting, obtain the item and confirm a changed detail without models; identify 3 audio facts. Relay a simple meeting message to a friend.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-08, DE-A1-07, DE-A1-13.
- **Return to review:** A previously learned component fails in the combined situation; queue that component as well as noting capstone evidence. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
