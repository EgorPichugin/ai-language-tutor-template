# DE-A1-08 — Transport and directions

- **Can-do:** Buy a basic ticket and understand a short route instruction.
- **Situation:** A station information desk in Vienna or Berlin.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Eine Fahrkarte nach …, bitte.; Wo ist Gleis …?; Muss ich umsteigen?; Gehen Sie geradeaus.; Links / rechts.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** nach/zu as travel chunks; müssen; imperative recognition; wo questions.
- **Listening goal:** A slow 20–30-second direction: identify platform and one action such as changing trains.
- **Speaking goal:** Ask destination/platform and repeat the key instruction in their own short words.
- **Practical success:** Request a ticket and follow two directions; identify platform and change/no-change from audio. Read a departure board separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-04, DE-A1-07.
- **Return to review:** The learner follows the wrong direction or cannot clarify a changed platform after prior success. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A1-08 — Transport and directions`
