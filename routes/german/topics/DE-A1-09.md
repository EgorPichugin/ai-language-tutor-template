# DE-A1-09 — Home and rooms

- **Can-do:** Describe a home and ask about a room.
- **Situation:** A simple room viewing or conversation with a neighbour.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Die Wohnung hat … Zimmer.; Die Küche ist klein.; Wo ist das Bad?; Der Tisch steht hier.; Wie hoch ist die Miete?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** es gibt + accusative; haben; room nouns; location chunks, no full two-way-preposition system yet.
- **Listening goal:** A slow room description: identify number of rooms and one feature.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe three rooms/features and ask a relevant viewing question.
- **Practical success:** Communicate three home facts and one question; identify 2 audio details from a different home.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-03, DE-A1-07.
- **Return to review:** Rooms, existence and possession are confused in repeated descriptions. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
