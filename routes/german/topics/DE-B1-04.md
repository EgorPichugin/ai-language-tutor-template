# DE-B1-04 — Explaining problems and solutions

- **Can-do:** Explain a familiar problem, its effect and a realistic next step.
- **Situation:** An everyday device or routine process stops working.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Das Problem ist, dass …; Dadurch kann ich … nicht.; Ich habe schon … versucht.; Wir könnten …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Dass/weil; dadurch/deshalb; Perfekt for attempted fixes; könnten/würden suggestions.
- **Listening goal:** A clear short problem report: identify issue, effect and attempted solution.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a structured one-minute report and respond to a clarification question.
- **Practical success:** Make problem/effect/action clear and propose one plausible next step; identify gist plus 3 audio facts.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-07, DE-A2-08, DE-B1-02.
- **Return to review:** The report omits the practical effect or the proposed solution is unclear without prompting. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
