# DE-A2-01 — Settling into a new place

- **Can-do:** Describe your surroundings and compare simple housing options.
- **Situation:** Discuss two flats and nearby services with a neighbour.
- **Chunks and patterns:** In der Nähe gibt es …; Die Wohnung ist größer als …; Ich wohne seit … hier.; Am liebsten …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Comparatives; dative location chunks; seit + present for ongoing residence.
- **Listening goal:** A clear slow 40-second description: identify area, transport access and one advantage.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe your area and compare two practical features; ask a follow-up.
- **Practical success:** Choose a place using two concrete features; identify 3 audio details and read a short listing separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-09, DE-A1-08.
- **Return to review:** Comparison or location errors repeatedly reverse the intended housing choice. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
