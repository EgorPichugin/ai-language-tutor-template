# DE-A2-03 — Talking about yesterday

- **Can-do:** Tell a short sequence of completed events.
- **Situation:** A colleague asks about yesterday or the weekend.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Gestern habe ich … gemacht.; Danach bin ich … gefahren.; Es war …; Am Ende …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Perfekt with haben/sein; frequent participles; war/hatte as common past forms; time connectors.
- **Listening goal:** A clear slow 45–60-second account: identify three actions and put them in order.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a five-sentence account with a start, sequence and ending; answer a follow-up.
- **Practical success:** Narrate three events in order and answer one unprepared question; identify/order 3 audio events.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-05, DE-A1-13.
- **Return to review:** The same auxiliary/participle error appears in separate sessions or a new past-event story requires a model. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
