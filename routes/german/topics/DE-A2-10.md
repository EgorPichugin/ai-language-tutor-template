# DE-A2-10 — Health appointments and routines

- **Can-do:** Describe simple symptoms over time and clarify an instruction.
- **Situation:** A fictional consultation or reception conversation.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Seit drei Tagen habe ich …; Es tut hier weh.; Wie oft soll ich …?; Habe ich Sie richtig verstanden?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Seit duration; modal verbs; reflexive sich fühlen; frequency expressions.
- **Listening goal:** A clear slow routine instruction: identify action, frequency and next appointment.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe a symptom timeline and repeat back an instruction to check understanding.
- **Practical success:** Communicate symptom/duration and ask one clarification; retrieve 3 audio details. The tutor practises language, not diagnosis or dosing advice.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-10, DE-A2-03, DE-A1-11.
- **Return to review:** Duration/frequency misunderstandings recur; return to language clarification practice without giving real medical advice. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A2-10 — Health appointments and routines`
