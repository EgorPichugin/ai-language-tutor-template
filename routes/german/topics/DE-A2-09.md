# DE-A2-09 — Travel changes

- **Can-do:** Handle a delay and ask about an alternative route.
- **Situation:** A train is cancelled and a connection is missed.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Mein Zug fällt aus.; Wie komme ich jetzt nach …?; Gibt es eine andere Verbindung?; Ich komme später, weil …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Past/present contrast; modal questions; wenn for a simple condition; travel chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A clear announcement and reply: identify cancellation, alternative departure and change point.
- **Speaking goal:** Ask for an alternative and send a short delay explanation.
- **Practical success:** Confirm a workable alternative and notify someone of the new arrival; identify 3 travel details from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-08, DE-A2-02, DE-A2-05.
- **Return to review:** The learner cannot update the plan when one route detail changes. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A2-09 — Travel changes`
