# DE-A1-01 — Meeting people

- **Can-do:** Introduce yourself and ask a new colleague their name.
- **Situation:** First meeting at a language group or workplace reception.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Guten Tag, ich heiße …; Ich komme aus …; Wie heißen Sie?; Freut mich.; Können Sie das bitte wiederholen?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** ich/Sie; sein and heißen in present tense; simple W-questions; formal Sie as a chunk.
- **Listening goal:** A slow 15–25-second introduction: identify the speaker’s name and place of origin (town or country).
- **Speaking goal:** Give three personal facts and ask one reciprocal question; practise name stress and spelling when needed.
- **Practical success:** Exchange a greeting, name and origin, then ask a question without reading a model; identify 2 audio facts.
- **Dependencies:** none.
- **Return to review:** Name/origin questions are confused in two sessions, or the learner cannot introduce themselves to a different partner. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
