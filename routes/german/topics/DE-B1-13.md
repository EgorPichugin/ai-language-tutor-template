# DE-B1-13 — Summarising and relaying information

- **Can-do:** Summarize a familiar message for someone who needs the key points.
- **Situation:** Explain a short workplace notice or local announcement to a colleague.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Es geht hauptsächlich um …; Wichtig ist, dass …; Für dich bedeutet das …; Die nächste Aktion ist …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Reported information without requiring Konjunktiv I; connectors; distinguishing source facts and personal opinion.
- **Listening goal:** A clear 90–120-second familiar report: identify main point, two details and required action.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a 60-second summary in simple German; relay necessary information across native/target language if useful.
- **Practical success:** Preserve main point/action and two supporting facts without copying whole sentences; identify gist and 3 audio details. Also summarize an original short written notice.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-13, DE-B1-01, DE-B1-06.
- **Return to review:** A summary adds unsupported facts or loses an action/time important to the recipient. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
