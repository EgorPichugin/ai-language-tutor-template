# DE-A1-02 — Personal details

- **Can-do:** Give and check basic details without sharing real private data.
- **Situation:** A fictional registration desk or course form.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich wohne in …; Meine Telefonnummer ist …; Wie schreibt man das?; Ist das richtig?; Meine Adresse ist …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Regular present verbs; alphabet; numbers 0–100; basic question order.
- **Listening goal:** A slowly dictated fictional name and phone number: pick out surname spelling and final four digits.
- **Speaking goal:** Spell a fictional surname, give a city and check one detail; distinguish common number endings.
- **Practical success:** Complete four fictional form fields and clarify one uncertain detail; retrieve the specified name/digits from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-01.
- **Return to review:** Spelling/numbers repeatedly change the intended detail, or checking a misunderstood detail needs a model. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A1-02 — Personal details`
