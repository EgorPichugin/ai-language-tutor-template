# DE-A2-12 — Colleagues and small talk

- **Can-do:** Maintain a short familiar conversation and ask follow-up questions.
- **Situation:** A lunch break, hobby group or team coffee.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Wie war dein Wochenende?; Wirklich?; Und dann?; Das kenne ich.; Was machst du gern, wenn …?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Question recycling; Perfekt; wenn; pronoun reference; conversational response chunks.
- **Listening goal:** Two people speak clearly and slowly: identify topic, one experience and whether they agree.
- **Speaking goal:** Keep a six-turn exchange going with two relevant follow-ups; allow pauses and repair.
- **Practical success:** Respond to a partner’s content and ask two non-scripted questions; identify topic/detail/agreement from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-03, DE-A2-05, DE-A1-13.
- **Return to review:** The conversation becomes isolated prepared statements or follow-up questions fail across sessions. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
