# DE-A1-06 — Food and ordering

- **Can-do:** Order food and ask a simple menu question.
- **Situation:** A café in Germany or Austria.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich hätte gern …; Für mich bitte …; Ist das mit Milch?; Die Rechnung, bitte.; Zum Mitnehmen, bitte.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** möchten / hätte gern as polite chunks; accusative common food nouns; ein/kein; no full subjunctive paradigm.
- **Listening goal:** A slow server reply: identify the available item and price.
- **Speaking goal:** Order two items, respond to an availability change, and ask for the bill.
- **Practical success:** Complete the order and bill request with no model; identify 2 audio details. Read two menu items as a separate reading task.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-02.
- **Return to review:** The learner cannot replace an unavailable item or a recurring negation error changes the order. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
