# DE-B1-06 — Meetings and joint planning

- **Can-do:** Take part in a familiar small-group plan and confirm responsibilities.
- **Situation:** Plan a team lunch, small release preparation or shared event.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich schlage vor, dass …; Wer übernimmt …?; Damit bin ich einverstanden.; Können wir festhalten, dass …?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Dass; modal verbs; purpose damit in simple clauses; agreeing/disagreeing chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A clear structured meeting excerpt: identify proposed plan, decision and owner.
- **Speaking goal:** Make one suggestion, respond to another and check who does what; ask for clarification when needed.
- **Practical success:** Help reach a shared plan with owner and next step; identify gist and 3 audio facts. Write a short decision note separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-B1-02, DE-A2-02, DE-A2-12.
- **Return to review:** Responsibilities remain ambiguous or a partner’s proposal cannot be answered beyond yes/no. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
