# DE-B1-12 — Service complaints and agreements

- **Can-do:** Make a clear polite complaint and negotiate a practical next step.
- **Situation:** A delayed service or repeated household repair problem.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich hatte vereinbart, dass …; Leider ist bisher …; Ich würde gern eine Lösung finden.; Können wir uns darauf einigen, dass …?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Common past agreement chunks; dass; polite subjunctive chunks; reasons and contrast.
- **Listening goal:** A clear service conversation: identify complaint, proposed remedy and deadline.
- **Speaking goal:** Explain chronology/effect, request a remedy and confirm a mutually understood next step.
- **Practical success:** Reach an agreement, or clearly state what remains unresolved and confirm the next action/date; identify gist and 3 audio facts. Write a concise follow-up message separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-08, DE-B1-04, DE-B1-05.
- **Return to review:** A complaint loses its sequence or an agreement is reported inaccurately after a new complication. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
