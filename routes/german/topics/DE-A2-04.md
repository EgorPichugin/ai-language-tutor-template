# DE-A2-04 — Work experience

- **Can-do:** Describe past roles and routine skills in simple terms.
- **Situation:** A new colleague asks about previous work.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich habe als … gearbeitet.; Ich war für … zuständig.; Ich kann …; Ich habe Erfahrung mit …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Perfekt of frequent work verbs; war/hatte; als/für/mit chunks; können for skills.
- **Listening goal:** A clear 45–60-second work history: identify role, duration and one task.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe one former role and two tasks; ask a simple experience question.
- **Practical success:** Give a coherent short role/task account with a real or fictional example; identify 3 audio facts. Write three CV-style lines separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-03, DE-A1-12.
- **Return to review:** The learner can list skills but cannot explain one past task without significant prompting. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A2-04 — Work experience`
