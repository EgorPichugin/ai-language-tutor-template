# DE-A2-14 — A practical week

- **Can-do:** Combine a past event, a current problem and a revised plan.
- **Situation:** A broken device changes work and weekend arrangements.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Letzte Woche habe ich …; Jetzt muss ich …; Deshalb schlage ich … vor.; Passt das für dich?
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Recycle Perfekt, reasons, modals and future present; no new grammar targets.
- **Listening goal:** Two clear short messages: identify problem, proposed solution and changed time.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe what happened, give a reason and negotiate a next step across linked exchanges.
- **Practical success:** Complete three practical exchanges and a short written confirmation; identify 3 audio facts and relay the plan. Queue weak component skills individually.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-08, DE-A2-09, DE-A2-12, DE-A2-13.
- **Return to review:** A familiar subskill breaks down when combined with another task, despite prior separate success. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
