# DE-A2-07 — Problems at home

- **Can-do:** Report a household fault and agree a repair visit.
- **Situation:** Tell a landlord about a broken heater using fictional details.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Die Heizung funktioniert nicht.; Seit gestern ist …; Könnten Sie jemanden schicken?; Ich bin ab … zu Hause.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Seit with present; subordinate clauses with weil; location prepositions at/in; polite request chunks.
- **Listening goal:** A clear slow reply: identify proposed repair day/time and an instruction.
- **Speaking goal:** Describe fault, duration and effect; negotiate access time.
- **Practical success:** Give three useful problem facts and agree a visit; recover 3 reply details. Draft a short repair request separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-01, DE-A2-05, DE-A1-11.
- **Return to review:** The listener cannot determine the fault or agreed access time from the learner’s message. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-A2-07 — Problems at home`
