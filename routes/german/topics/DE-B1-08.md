# DE-B1-08 — Professional experience

- **Can-do:** Describe your contribution to a past work result.
- **Situation:** Networking or a first conversation with a potential employer.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Meine Aufgabe war …; Ich habe dabei …; Das Ergebnis war …; Dabei habe ich gelernt, … zu …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Past narration; relative clauses for simple descriptions; zu-infinitive; result connectors.
- **Listening goal:** A clear work account: identify role, action and result; distinguish individual from team contribution.
- **Speaking goal:** Give a 90-second role/action/result example and answer two follow-ups, one at a time.
- **Practical success:** Explain a concrete contribution without inventing qualifications; identify gist and 3 work details from audio. Read a short job ad separately.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A2-04, DE-A2-11, DE-B1-01.
- **Return to review:** The account reverts to a list of job titles or ownership/result cannot be clarified. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
- **Thematic chat:** `DE-B1-08 — Professional experience`
