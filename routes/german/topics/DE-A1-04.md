# DE-A1-04 — Time and appointments

- **Can-do:** Arrange a simple appointment using a day and clock time.
- **Situation:** A course reception desk offers two appointments.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Wann haben Sie Zeit?; Am Montag um zehn Uhr.; Passt Ihnen das?; Dann bis morgen.; Der Termin ist am …
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Days/months; clock times; am/um; present-tense questions; ordinal dates as chunks.
- **Listening goal:** Two slowly offered times: identify the chosen day and time; introduce official 24-hour time before colloquial alternatives.
- **Speaking goal:** Ask availability, accept one time and repeat it clearly.
- **Practical success:** Complete a three-turn booking and confirm the day/time correctly; retrieve both from audio.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-02.
- **Return to review:** Day/time reversals cause a missed arrangement or asking for confirmation again needs sentence starters. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
