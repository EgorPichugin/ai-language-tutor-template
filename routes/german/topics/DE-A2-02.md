# DE-A2-02 — Plans and arrangements

- **Can-do:** Make a plan and change it with a simple explanation.
- **Situation:** Arrange a weekend visit or a work appointment.
- **Chunks and patterns:** Ich habe vor, … zu …; Morgen fahre ich …; Können wir den Termin verschieben?; Das passt mir besser.
- **Grammar in service of the task:** Present for future; modal verbs; common zu-infinitive plan chunk; time-first word order.
- **Listening goal:** A short clear planning exchange: identify original time, new time and place.
- **Speaking goal:** Propose a plan, handle one unavailable time and confirm an alternative.
- **Practical success:** Agree a revised plan in five turns without sentence models; recover 3 audio facts.
- **Dependencies:** DE-A1-13, DE-A1-04.
- **Return to review:** The revised time is lost or a plan cannot be renegotiated after earlier independence. Also apply the shared regression and spacing rules in INSTRUCTIONS.md.
