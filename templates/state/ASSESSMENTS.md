## Entry shape (template, not a result)

Record: assessment ID; local date; kind (diagnostic / seven_lesson / monthly / combined); trigger(s); completed lesson count covered and seven-lesson threshold(s) covered; task topics; completed yes/no; relevant previous assessment for comparison.

For each task include the actual prompt/scenario and short response, task conditions and result, independence, significant_errors, listening, spoken_output, reuse, transfer and retention; audio source/plays/transcript exposure when relevant. Diagnostic tasks have reuse/retention not_assessed until genuinely tested. Give separate listening, spoken production, interaction, reading and writing estimates with confidence/evidence; record mediation observations when sampled. Unobserved skills stay not_assessed. Finish with concrete strengths, gaps, route recommendation and limits. Never present the estimate as a certificate or exam score.
