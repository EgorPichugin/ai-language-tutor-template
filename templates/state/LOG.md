## Entry shape (template, not a completed session)

For each session record: ID; local date; mode; planned/actual duration (unknown if unmeasured); main topic; review topics; measurable goal; completed yes/no; save_state pending/complete.

For each assessed topic record the prompt/scenario, short actual response/evidence, task length and result, independence, significant_errors, listening, spoken_output, reuse, transfer and retention. Include audio source/scenario, duration, plays, transcript exposure and answers when listening is assessed. Use not_assessed explicitly for missing observations. Include up to three correction targets with exact learner phrase, natural replacement and short rule. Final check and homework outcome must reflect what actually happened.

While pending, list concrete intended file updates keyed by IDs; on successful reread replace that list with a short completion summary. Reference Axxx for assessment evidence instead of duplicating its responses here.
