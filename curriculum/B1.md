# B1 transferable curriculum

Connected speech on familiar matters, clear standard audio and simple spontaneous follow-ups; occasional repair remains normal.

Stories, opinions, problem solving and familiar work conversations. Reading/writing: straightforward notices, job ads and connected practical messages. Mediation: summarize essential familiar information for another person.

This framework maps communicative purposes to the fully specified German cards. A different language must replace phrases, grammar, pronunciation and cultural details; do not merely relabel German content. Each linked card includes situation, patterns, grammar, listening, speaking, success, dependencies and return conditions.

## Level boundary and progression

Use the level capstone to sample combined skills, then check the separate skill profile and evidence gaps. Do not require flawless grammar, do not equate finishing cards with a CEFR certificate, and do not hide unassessed listening.
