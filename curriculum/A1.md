# A1 transferable curriculum

Familiar, predictable exchanges with a supportive partner, short sentences and slow input.

Names, everyday needs, time, home, services and a first workplace exchange. Reading/writing: signs, menus, form fields and short messages. Mediation: relay a meeting time or simple instruction.

This framework maps communicative purposes to the fully specified German cards. A different language must replace phrases, grammar, pronunciation and cultural details; do not merely relabel German content. Each linked card includes situation, patterns, grammar, listening, speaking, success, dependencies and return conditions.

## Level boundary and progression

Use the level capstone to sample combined skills, then check the separate skill profile and evidence gaps. Do not require flawless grammar, do not equate finishing cards with a CEFR certificate, and do not hide unassessed listening. Progression and status rules live only in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](../INSTRUCTIONS.md).
