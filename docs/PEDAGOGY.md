# Teaching rationale

This is a practical learning design, not a validated treatment or an official syllabus. The operational rules live only in [INSTRUCTIONS.md](../INSTRUCTIONS.md); this document explains the choices.

## Learn actions in situations

“Arrange a different appointment time” gives the learner a purpose, a listener and an observable outcome. Sentence chunks support that action; grammar is introduced when it helps communicate. The same purpose returns with new details so a memorized line is not mistaken for transferable ability.

Understandable input comes before an independent response, followed by selective correction and reuse. A learner who says “Ich habe nach Hause gefahren” can compare it with “Ich bin nach Hause gefahren”, then use the corrected pattern for a different journey. Correcting a small number of meaningful targets protects the flow of conversation while making the next attempt more deliberate.

## Separate learning from measuring

A model sentence is helpful during practice, but a successful imitation is not an independent exit task. Likewise, a fluent answer after a refresher does not demonstrate retention. Delayed checks begin cold, before teaching. The tutor preserves those distinctions in evidence rather than inferring mastery from confidence or tone.

Listening requires audio. Reading the same message is a different activity. Typed role-play rehearses useful language, but it does not establish pronunciation, auditory comprehension or live spoken fluency. A course can continue in text while explicitly recording these gaps. A fully assessed topic is stricter than a partial written gain.

## Keep a broad language profile

The focus is interaction, speaking and listening, with small reading/writing tasks woven into practical situations. Mediation means helping someone else understand information: for example, passing on the time and action from a notice in simple language. It is not restricted to literal translation.

The CEFR provides communicative reference scales rather than prescribing this template's 42 topics, German grammar sequence, error limits or review intervals. The [source crosswalk](SOURCES.md) distinguishes those references from author decisions. Uneven skills are expected: a learner may read familiar notices more confidently than they understand a phone call.

## Review without trapping the learner

The queue preserves specific weak skills and evidence instead of resetting an entire level. Brief retrieval can accompany new topics; a mixed review connects a few old ones. If the queue grows, the tutor prioritizes and adjusts the plan instead of presenting an endless backlog. Return after a break means fresh observation, not automatic loss of every achievement.

The 3/7/30-day spacing and practice thresholds are transparent starting heuristics. Their effectiveness for a particular learner is not established here. Teachers should tune them after observing workload, retention and accessibility needs. More practice with real speakers and varied audio is necessary to evaluate transfer beyond this AI setting.
