## Council of Europe

1. [CEFR Companion Volume (2020), official PDF](https://rm.coe.int/cefr-companion-volume-with-new-descriptors-2020/16809ea0d4). Relevant printed pages: profiles and descriptor use pp.38–43; oral comprehension p.48; oral production and experience pp.62–65; oral interaction pp.72–80; online interaction pp.84–87; mediation pp.90–122. The 2020 publication date is the edition date, regardless of a search engine's more recent crawl label.
2. [Official CEFR descriptor search](https://www.coe.int/en/web/common-european-framework-reference-languages/cefr-descriptors-search). A way to inspect relevant scale/level combinations directly; not a prescribed German grammar syllabus.

Compact paraphrase of the reference logic: A1 input/exchanges are short and strongly supported; A2 deals with routine practical information and simple accounts; B1 includes connected familiar messages, reasons and practical cooperation under clear-language conditions. Skill profiles can be uneven. The source does not prescribe our numerical success rules.

## Curriculum crosswalk — author interpretation

| Reference area | A1 route emphasis | A2 route emphasis | B1 route emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Oral reception | Slow introductions, concrete times/prices and short directions | Clear short accounts, phone messages and routine notices | Gist and relevant detail in clear familiar narratives, meetings and reports |
| Production and interaction | Basic personal facts, requests and predictable exchanges | Short experience accounts, plans, reasons and routine repairs | Connected stories, practical opinions, problems and familiar work exchanges |
| Reading and writing | Menus, signs, forms, brief confirmations | Notices, simple messages and routine service correspondence | Job ads, straightforward reports and connected practical follow-up |
| Mediation | Relay a time or simple instruction | Pass on appointment essentials and a proposed plan | Summarize familiar information for a colleague and preserve action points |

The crosswalk explains intent, not a validated alignment claim. Fourteen blocks per level, the German grammar order, task lengths, error counts, status labels and review intervals are not official requirements. Work/software/KI topics use familiar examples and short explanations; specialist argumentation is outside the intended scope.
