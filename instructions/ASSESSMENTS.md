# Diagnostics, checkpoints and return

Return to [the core](../INSTRUCTIONS.md) for teaching/evidence rules and use [STATE.md](STATE.md) for saves. Assessment evidence belongs in ASSESSMENTS, not LOG lesson evidence.

## Initial diagnostic, 10–15 minutes

Ask one low-pressure task at a time. Begin with self-introduction/simple exchange; a complete beginner may say “I don't know”, then learn one phrase after the attempt. If independent performance supports it, sample a past/planning exchange, then connected opinion/problem explanation. Stop escalation after repeated difficulty. Separately sample a short reading notice and written reply. Use actual audio if available under the core rules.

Record A001 prompts, actual short responses, modality, support, errors, result and uncertainty. Give separate listening, spoken production, interaction, reading and writing estimates with confidence; mediation if sampled. Untested skills are `not_assessed`; untouched topics are `not_started`. Self-report or one success is not a global level. Diagnostic evidence cannot establish retention or stable.

## Due checks

- Every-seven check is due when the largest reached threshold `7 × floor(completed lessons / 7)` (at least 7) lacks a completed assessment. It remains due at counts 8, 9, etc. Record covered threshold(s) and actual count.
- Monthly is due 30 days after the initial diagnostic or last completed monthly check.
- If both are due, one combined assessment covers both. Missed thresholds need one current check, not a backlog.
- Learner time/preferences may defer a check; PLAN records reason and next opportunity.

Every-seven, 15–20 minutes: use two or three representative recent tasks plus an older weak topic; sample oral input/interaction and brief reading/writing. Measure before correction.

Monthly, 20–30 minutes: fresh situations across at least three studied themes; sample listening, speaking, interaction, reading/writing and simple mediation. Compare like evidence, not just percentages.

Return after 21+ days, 15–20 minutes: ask one brief goals/time question, then cold-sample two or three formerly usable topics, or fewer previously attempted topics if that is all. Lower load, queue observed weaknesses, preserve demonstrated skills. Time passing alone does not downgrade everything. If monthly/checkpoint is also due, combine them and record all triggers.

Use the core rubric. Correction follows measurement. A checkpoint is not a counted lesson. Save incomplete work honestly; do not invent unperformed modalities/tasks. Finish with concrete strengths, gaps, next route action and limits, then apply the core closing/handoff rules.
