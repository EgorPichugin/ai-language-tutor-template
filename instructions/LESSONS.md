# Lesson procedures — load for lessons

Return to [the core](../INSTRUCTIONS.md) for teaching, evidence, status and closing rules. Generate material from the selected topic card, relevant PROFILE/state and evidence; do not use prewritten examples as learner data.

At start, resolve pending saves first. Priority: return after 21+ days → due checkpoint → due review affecting today's goal → next ready PLAN topic. A requested explicit topic overrides the recommendation; briefly note missing prerequisites and use a bridge/smaller task rather than blocking the course.

Count a lesson only when LOG mode is `full`, `short`, `topic_review`, `mixed_review` or `return` and `completed: yes`. A later-finished partial updates its existing ID; it is not appended again. Setup, diagnostic and checkpoints do not count.

| Mode | Budget and sequence |
| --- | --- |
| Full, 60 min | 5 retrieval; 5 goal/context; 10 input/listening; 15 interaction; 8 correction/reuse; 7 fresh final task; 5 reading/writing/mediation; 5 homework/save |
| Short, 15–20 min | 2 retrieval; 4 input/check; 6 exchange/correction/reuse; 3 final task; remainder homework/save and optional brief literacy task |
| Topic review, 15 min | Cold attempt before refresh; targeted input; repair/reuse; new-situation final task; save |
| Mixed review, 20–30 min | Two or three due topics in one realistic situation; cold attempt, repair, transfer; score topics separately and count one session |

Use one main topic and one measurable can-do goal. Retrieve one or two old topics (none for a true first lesson). Include valid audio where feasible, independent interaction, at most three main corrections, changed-detail reuse, a no-hint final task, small homework and verified save. Timings are planning budgets, not measured elapsed time; reduce a short lesson's goal rather than evidence standards. Let the learner stop early.

Review queue rules: one open row per topic/focus. New usable → due in 3 days; successful 3-day retrieval → 7 days later; qualifying stable → 30 days; later stable reviews about every 30 days. Failure/recurrent error → `next_session`; repaired usable restarts at 3 days. `modality_gap` waits for audio availability and must not flood lessons. These are adjustable heuristics.

Prioritize regression/prerequisites, then oldest due items. Ordinary retrieval uses about 20% of a lesson and at most two topics. If many are overdue, offer a mixed review of at most three and reschedule the rest with reasons. A weak topic does not indefinitely block unrelated learning.

For a topic review, use the exact code/title from TOPICS and the explicit command/current-chat binding. For mixed review, allocate/reuse the next `DE-R01 — Mixed review` style title in PLAN, changing the language prefix as needed. After the final task apply the core rubric and closing decision, save through [STATE.md](STATE.md), and load [HANDOFF.md](HANDOFF.md) only if a destination is needed.
