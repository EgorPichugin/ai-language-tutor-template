# A1 short lesson — text-only illustration

Topic: `DE-A1-01 — Meeting people`. Duration budget: 20 minutes. Goal: greet a new colleague, give name/origin, ask their name. Assume a fresh learner has only completed setup; there are no old topics to retrieve, so revisit the attempted diagnostic greeting briefly.

**First independent turn:** “Ты знакомишься с коллегой. Представься по-немецки.” Wait. Suppose the typed response is “Ich heißen Alex. Ich komme aus Polen.”

**After the response:** “Ich heißen Alex → Ich heiße Alex. С ich здесь нужна форма heiße.” Then one task: “Теперь представься другому коллеге и спроси его имя.” Wait; introduce the question chunk only if the learner cannot attempt it. A supplied chunk makes that attempt supported.

**Input:** No sound is available. Say so, use an original two-line written introduction for a reading check, and ask one question at a time. Label this reading, not listening. Do not show a script and then count its comprehension as audio evidence.

**Reuse:** Change the partner and origin. Remove the model. Let the learner answer fully before any correction.

**Final check:** A new reception scenario requires greeting, three facts and a reciprocal question without visible sentence starters. Record actual typed result, help and errors. Even if it succeeds, spoken_output and listening are not_assessed. Topic stays learning, with written-transfer evidence and a single waiting_for_audio review item.

**Homework:** Three minutes: recall the introduction aloud without reading full sentences. The next session checks it if audio input is available; self-report alone does not establish speaking success.
